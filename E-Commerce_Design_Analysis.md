## 1. NEW LEAF PRODUCTS (new-leaf-products.com)

### Homepage Layout & Structure
- **Hero Section**: Prominent headline "High-quality supplements. _For everyone._" with 3 featured category tiles (Shop Men, Shop Women, Shop Vegan)
- **Featured Collections**: Four collection cards below hero (Vitamins & Minerals, Health Goals, Vitamin Gummies, Health Bundles)
- **Quiz CTA**: "Not sure where to start? TAKE THE QUIZ" - engages uncertain visitors with personalized pathway
- **Blog Section**: Three featured health articles (dull skin, focus, sleep remedies) build authority
- **Promotional Bar**: Top-level messaging about same-day dispatch (before 4pm) and discount tiers (Buy 2: 10% OFF, Buy 3: 15% OFF)

### Navigation
- **Header Structure**: Multi-level dropdown system with primary categories
  - Vitamins & Minerals
  - Health Goals
  - Bundles
  - Health Quiz
  - Customer Care
- **Country/Region Selector**: Currency options across 13 locations
- **Secondary Navigation**: Account login and shopping cart in top-right corner
- **Logo**: Links to homepage from anywhere on site

### Product Pages
- **Collection Page Approach**: Navigation hub rather than product showcase
- **Categories by Two Models**: 
  1. Ingredient-based (Ashwagandha, Biotin, Calcium, etc.) - 20+ supplement types
  2. Benefit-focused (Bones & Joints, Brain Health, Energy, Sleep & Relaxation, etc.)
- **Supply Duration Filtering**: Collections organized by supply duration (1-month through 12-month options)

### Category/Collection Pages
- **Primary Trust Signal**: "30-Day Money Back Guarantee" prominently featured
- **Trustpilot Integration**: Review badge linked throughout
- **Collection Organization**: Extensive taxonomy without visible on-page filtering shown
- **Product Card Elements** (from homepage):
  - Product image
  - Product name
  - Rating display
  - Pricing (original and sale)
  - Supply duration
  - Brief benefit description
  - "Add to basket" button

### Footer
- **Company Info Section**: About the business
- **Support Links**: My Account, FAQs, Contact Us
- **Information Sections**: About Us, Terms & Conditions
- **Payment Methods**: Icons displayed for multiple payment options
- **Social Media**: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok links

### SEO & Trust Elements
- **30-Day Money Back Guarantee**: Central trust mechanism
- **100% Customer Satisfaction** badge
- **Trustpilot Reviews**: Linked and branded throughout
- **Same-Day Dispatch Promise**: Time-based trust builder
- **Free Delivery**: Within 2 business days

### Design Patterns
- **Color Scheme**: Light backgrounds with dark logo and text (clean, clinical aesthetic)
- **Typography**: Bold section headings emphasizing benefits and features
- **Grid Layout**: Modular, horizontal card sections
- **Responsive Design**: Adapts across devices
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Benefits emphasized over ingredient complexity

### UX Flows
- **Discovery Pathway 1**: Quiz-based personalization ("Not sure where to start?")
- **Discovery Pathway 2**: Browse by ingredient or health goal
- **Promotional Incentive**: Multi-item discounts encourage basket building
- **Time Urgency**: Same-day dispatch messaging for immediate gratification

### Overall Aesthetic
- **Positioning**: Accessible premium (high quality at reasonable price)
- **Target Audience Signals**: Busy professionals seeking convenience, health-conscious individuals, inclusive approach (Men, Women, Vegan categories)
- **Feel**: Trustworthy, clinical, straightforward

## KEY RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SCLERO PROTEINS LTD

### Homepage Strategy
1. **Lead with Social Proof** (customer count or clinical backing - position as authority in protein category)
2. **Feature Hero Promotion** (immediate value prop: free shipping, first-order discount, or speed)
3. **Include Quiz/Personalization** (help visitors find ideal protein type for their goal: muscle gain, recovery, athletic performance, etc.)
4. **Show 10-12 Featured Products** in grid below hero
5. **Include Educational Content** (blog articles, testimonials, or clinical validation for protein benefits)

### Navigation Architecture
```
Main Menu:
├── Shop All Proteins
├── Shop by Protein Type (Whey, Plant-Based, Casein, Blends, etc.)
├── Shop by Goal (Muscle Gain, Recovery, Energy, Weight Management, etc.)
├── Bundles
├── Learn (Blog, Science, Testimonials)
└── Personalization Quiz
```

### Product Card Standards
```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│  Product Image (16:9)   │
├─────────────────────────┤
│ Protein Name            │
│ ★★★★★ 4.85/5 (427 reviews) │

│ 30g protein / 2.2kg     │

│ protein | Vegan-friendly"│
│                         │
│   Add to Basket →       │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

### Product Detail Page Essentials
1. **Multi-angle product gallery** (packaging + lifestyle imagery)
2. **Clear pricing** (one-time + subscription option if applicable)
3. **Protein specs front-and-center** (protein per serving, amino acid profile, dietary fit)
4. **Transparent reviews** with rating breakdown
5. **Ingredient table** with full transparency
6. **Trust badges** (certifications, third-party testing, guarantees)
7. **Related products** (flavor variants, complementary products)
8. **Subscription incentive** (offer % discount for recurring orders)

### Trust Signal Priorities for Protein Category
1. **Third-party testing certification** (NSF, Informed Sport, etc.) - critical for fitness audience
2. **Clinical backing** (study results, performance claims supported by research)
3. **Money-back guarantee** (30 days minimum)
4. **Ingredient transparency** (no hidden fillers, clear amino acid profiles)
5. **Sustainability certification** (if positioning supports it)
6. **Testimonials from fitness professionals** (athletes, trainers, nutritionists)

### Color & Design Considerations
- **Protein positioning** typically leans athletic/performance (bold colors, strong typography)
- **Consider spectrum**:
  - Performance-focused: Bold colors, dynamic imagery, technical specs prominent
  - Wellness-focused: Softer palette, lifestyle imagery, benefit-focused
  - Premium positioning: Sophisticated color (dark with accent), professional imagery
- **All sites use sufficient white space** - don't overcrowd product cards or pages

### Critical UX Elements
1. **Supply size clarity** (grams per container, servings per container) - parallel to supplement "supply duration"
2. **Flavor/variant selection** (dropdowns on product page for flavor, size, etc.)
3. **Filter capability on collection pages** (price range, dietary (vegan/keto/gluten-free), flavor, size)
4. **Sort options** (popularity, price low-high, rating, newest)
5. **Breadcrumb navigation** (Home > Shop > Whey Protein > Vanilla > Product Name)
6. **Mobile-optimized search** (search is critical for large product catalogs)

### Footer Architecture

### Checkout Indicators (Not Fully Analyzed But Expected)
